How perfume
develops.

A perfume can smell different an hour after you put it on. Here’s what to pay attention to when you try one.

First impression

Notice the first few minutes. Bright citrus, green, or airy notes often speak first.

The heart

Once the opening settles, see which character emerges. It might feel floral, fruity, or spiced.

The dry-down

Come back to it later. Woods, amber, and musk are examples of notes you may notice as the scent fades.
